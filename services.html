10 Comprehensive Book Marketing Services Under One Roof

Full Service Book Marketing From Pre Launch Planning Through Long Term Sustainable Growth.

Every service you need to launch your book, build your audience, generate reviews, drive sales, and scale your author career, all managed by a single trusted strategist who handles everything through clear, documented email communication.

🚀

1. Book Launch Strategy and Management

Your launch window is the single most critical period in your book's entire life cycle. The Amazon algorithm pays extremely close attention to your first 30 days on the platform. The sales velocity you achieve, the number and quality of reviews you accumulate, the keyword rankings you establish, and the category placement you secure during this initial window will determine your book's organic visibility for months or even years to come.

Get your launch right, and the algorithm rewards you with organic visibility in search results, recommendation placements on product pages, inclusion in also bought carousels, and sustained sales momentum that compounds over time. Get it wrong, and your book disappears into the void of over 12 million other titles currently available on Amazon.

  • Pre launch audience building and buzz generation across social media, email, and reader communities starting 90 days before publication.
  • ARC team recruitment, vetting, distribution, and review management through premium platforms including BookSirens, BookSprout, and NetGalley.
  • Launch week coordination including pricing strategy, promotional stacking across multiple deal sites, email blast scheduling, and real time performance monitoring.
  • Amazon category and keyword optimization for maximum discoverability in both fiction and non fiction categories.
  • Post launch momentum strategies to sustain your ranking and sales velocity long after launch week ends.
  • Goodreads Listopia nomination and voting campaigns timed to coincide with your launch for maximum visibility.
  • Book Club Listopia outreach to secure early book club picks that drive bulk sales during your critical launch window.
Best For: Authors who are 2 to 4 months away from publication and want a proven, comprehensive, stress free launch plan that covers every possible angle.
💵

2. Amazon Advertising and PPC Management

Amazon Ads are the single most powerful paid advertising tool for selling books on the planet. When done correctly, they put your book directly in front of readers who are actively searching for their next read at the exact moment they are ready to make a purchase decision. No other advertising platform offers this level of purchase intent targeting.

Most authors who attempt to run their own campaigns end up burning through their budgets with poorly targeted keywords, unoptimized bids, and zero negative keyword strategy. I handle every single aspect of your Amazon Advertising so you never have to worry about wasted spend.

  • Full campaign architecture setup across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and Lockscreen Ads.
  • Deep keyword research using premium tools including Publisher Rocket, Helium 10, and Amazon search term reports.
  • Strategic bid management with daily monitoring and optimization to ensure you never overpay for clicks.
  • A/B testing of ad copy, headlines, custom images, and creative assets to continuously improve conversion rates.
  • Aggressive negative keyword implementation to eliminate wasted spend on irrelevant searches.
  • Detailed weekly performance reports delivered directly to your Gmail inbox with clear ROI metrics.
Best For: Authors who want consistent, scalable, and profitable book sales through paid advertising on the world's largest book marketplace.
📱

3. Social Media Marketing and BookTok Strategy

Social media has fundamentally changed how readers discover books. BookTok alone has generated billions of views and is directly responsible for reviving backlist titles, creating overnight bestsellers, and building massive reader communities around specific tropes and genres.

Going viral on social media is not about luck. It is about understanding algorithms, knowing your target audience, and creating content that hooks viewers in the first two seconds. I build platform specific content strategies for every major channel.

  • TikTok and BookTok: Trend participation, hook driven video concepts, trope based content series, and strategic hashtag usage.
  • Instagram: Reels, carousel posts, Stories, and community engagement strategies that build your author brand.
  • Facebook: Reader group engagement, targeted ad campaigns, and community building strategies.
  • X: Author community building, hashtag campaign development, and real time engagement.
  • YouTube: Book trailers, author vlogs, and long form videos optimized for search engine visibility.
Best For: Authors who want to build a loyal, engaged reader community that buys every book they release.
📧

4. Email Marketing and Reader Funnels

Your email list is the single most valuable asset in your entire author business. Social media algorithms change overnight without warning. Amazon rankings fluctuate daily. But your email list belongs to you forever. An engaged email list of 5,000 active readers will consistently outperform a social media following of 50,000 when it comes to actual book sales.

  • Reader magnet creation tailored to your genre, including bonus chapters, prequel novellas, short stories, workbooks, and resource guides.
  • High converting landing page design and lead generation strategy.
  • Welcome email sequences that build trust, establish your author voice, and turn casual subscribers into superfans.
  • Launch email sequences of 7 to 12 emails sent over a 14 day period to drive massive day one sales.
  • Nurture campaigns that keep your list warm, engaged, and ready to buy between book releases.
  • Automated funnel setup using ConvertKit, MailerLite, Mailchimp, or ActiveCampaign.
Best For: Authors who want long term, sustainable, algorithm proof book sales that do not depend on the unpredictable whims of social media.
⭐

5. ARC Distribution and Review Campaigns

Reviews are the lifeblood of book sales on Amazon. Books with 50 or more verified reviews convert at dramatically higher rates than books with fewer than 10. Readers trust other readers, and a strong review count is what triggers the Amazon algorithm to start recommending your book organically to new readers.

  • ARC team recruitment, vetting, and management to ensure your book reaches genuine, active reviewers.
  • Distribution through premium platforms including BookSirens, BookSprout, and NetGalley.
  • Custom review request email sequences that maximize response rates without violating Amazon terms of service.
  • Full compliance monitoring to protect your account and ensure every review sticks.
  • Post launch review momentum strategies for evergreen titles and backlist catalog growth.
Best For: Authors launching a new book or looking to boost the review count on an existing title that has stalled.
🎤

6. PR, Media, and Podcast Outreach

Getting your book featured in the right media outlets can catapult your visibility in front of thousands of new readers almost overnight. A single well placed podcast interview or blog feature can drive more sales and generate more long term visibility than weeks of paid advertising.

  • Professional press release writing and distribution to targeted media outlets in your niche.
  • Custom media kit creation that positions you as a credible, newsworthy author.
  • Targeted blog tour coordination with genre specific reviewers and bloggers.
  • Podcast pitching and booking across shows with audiences of 10,000 to over 100,000 listeners.
  • Influencer and Bookstagrammer outreach campaigns for authentic social proof.
  • Literary award submission strategy and coordination.
Best For: Authors who want mainstream visibility, credibility, and authority that extends far beyond the Amazon ecosystem.
📷

7. Professional Book Mockup Design

In the world of online book sales, first impressions are everything. A flat, two dimensional cover image simply does not have the same psychological impact as a stunning, realistic 3D mockup that makes your book look like a physical object readers can reach out and hold. Studies consistently show that 3D mockups increase click through rates by up to 40 percent compared to flat cover images.

  • Single book mockups featuring hardcover, paperback, and eBook formats on clean, professional backgrounds.
  • Series bundle mockups that showcase your entire series together in a visually striking arrangement.
  • eReader and tablet mockups displaying your book on Kindle, iPad, and mobile devices.
  • Lifestyle mockups placing your book in real world settings like coffee shops, cozy reading nooks, and bookshelves.
  • Social media optimized mockups sized specifically for Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and TikTok.
  • Website hero mockups designed to be the centerpiece of your author website.
Best For: Authors who want to dramatically improve the visual appeal of their marketing materials and increase conversions across all platforms.
🎬

8. Cinematic Book Trailer Production

A book trailer is the single most powerful visual marketing asset you can create for your book. Think of it as a movie trailer for your novel. In 30 to 90 seconds, a well produced trailer can capture the mood, tone, and emotional core of your story in a way that no written description ever could.

  • Scriptwriting and storyboarding to distill your story into a compelling narrative that hooks viewers in the first 3 seconds.
  • Voiceover casting and recording with professional voice actors matched to your genre.
  • Music selection and licensing from premium royalty free libraries.
  • Visual editing using high quality footage, custom typography, and cinematic transitions.
  • Sound design and mixing for a polished, movie grade audio experience.
  • Final delivery in multiple aspect ratios: 16:9 for YouTube and websites, 9:16 for TikTok and Reels, and 1:1 for Facebook.
Best For: Authors who want a show stopping visual asset that builds pre launch excitement and continues driving sales long after publication day.
📖

9. Goodreads Listopia Placement Campaigns

Goodreads is the largest social network for readers in the world, with over 90 million active members. Listopia lists are user generated collections organized around specific themes, tropes, and genres. When your book appears on high traffic lists, it gets exposed to thousands of motivated readers who are actively looking for their next read.

  • Comprehensive research to identify the highest traffic Listopia lists in your genre.
  • Strategic nomination of your book to the most impactful and relevant lists.
  • Voting campaign coordination to move your book into top ranking positions.
  • Ongoing monitoring and maintenance to protect your rankings over time.
  • Detailed email reporting showing lists, rankings, and estimated traffic generated.
Best For: Authors who want long term, compounding organic visibility on the world's largest reader platform without relying solely on paid ads.
📚

10. Book Club Listopia and Outreach Campaigns

When a book club selects your book, you are not just selling one copy. You are selling 15, 30, 50, or 100 copies at once. Even better, every member becomes a potential advocate who recommends your book to friends, family, and coworkers.

  • Identification of active book club communities across Goodreads, Facebook Groups, and Reddit.
  • Crafting personalized pitches tailored to each club's specific reading preferences.
  • Direct outreach to organizers and selection committees.
  • Creation of professional book club discussion guides with questions and bonus content.
  • Bulk purchase coordination and guidance for club orders.
  • Detailed reporting on confirmed selections and reader feedback.
Best For: Authors who want to tap into the powerful world of group reads, bulk purchases, and organic word of mouth promotion.
