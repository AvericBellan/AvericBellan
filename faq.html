Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about my book marketing services, my process, my pricing, my email only communication methods, and what you can expect when you hire me to manage your book's campaign.

Pricing and Packages

How much do your book marketing services cost? +

Every campaign I build is custom tailored to your specific goals, genre, timeline, and budget. Most of my clients invest between $1,500 and $5,000 per month depending on the scope and complexity of their campaign. I will provide a detailed, transparent written quote via email after reviewing your initial inquiry. There are no hidden fees or surprise charges.

Do you offer one time project pricing or only monthly retainers? +

I offer both. For authors who need a specific, one time service such as a book mockup design, a book trailer production, a single Goodreads Listopia campaign, or a one time ARC distribution, I offer fixed project pricing. For authors who need ongoing advertising management, social media management, and email funnels, I offer monthly retainer packages.

Do I need a large budget to work with you? +

Not at all. I work with authors at a wide range of budget levels, from debut authors with modest budgets to established writers with large ad spends. A well executed $500 per month plan will always outperform a poorly managed $5,000 per month plan.

What payment methods do you accept? +

I accept payments via bank transfer, PayPal, and major credit cards. Payment terms and schedules will be clearly outlined in your written service agreement delivered via email.

Services and Expertise

Do you work with all genres? +

Yes. I have successfully managed campaigns across fiction genres (romance, thriller, mystery, fantasy, science fiction, horror, literary fiction, young adult) and non fiction categories (self help, business, memoir, health, spirituality, true crime). The core principles of data driven book marketing apply across all categories.

Can you help if my book is already published? +

Absolutely. Many of my most successful campaigns have been for books that were published months or even years ago. It is never too late to revitalize your book's visibility through Amazon Ads, Goodreads Listopia, and Book Club outreach.

Do you offer standalone Book Mockup Design or Book Trailer Production? +

Yes. Both Book Mockup Design and Book Trailer Production are available as individual, standalone services. Mockups are delivered in 3 to 5 business days, while trailers take 2 to 4 weeks from script to final delivery.

What is the difference between Goodreads Listopia and Book Club Listopia? +

Goodreads Listopia campaigns focus on getting your book voted onto popular lists on Goodreads for organic discovery among 90 million readers. Book Club Listopia campaigns focus specifically on getting your book selected by active reading clubs for group reads, driving bulk purchases of 15 to 100 copies at once.

Process and Communication

Why do you only communicate through Gmail? +

I conduct all communication exclusively through Gmail for three reasons: organization, accountability, and your convenience. Everything is in writing, searchable, and stored permanently. You never have to worry about forgetting notes from a call, and you can read and reply on your own schedule across any time zone.

How quickly will you respond to my emails? +

I respond to all client emails within 24 hours, Monday through Friday. Urgent matters regarding active ad campaigns are typically addressed within a few hours.

How often will I receive updates on my campaign? +

You will receive detailed weekly email updates summarizing progress, optimizations, and upcoming tasks, plus comprehensive monthly performance reports with full ROI metrics and analytics.

Results and Expectations

How soon will I see results? +

Most clients see measurable improvements within the first 30 days. Amazon Ads campaigns typically show initial traction within the first week, while organic strategies like Goodreads Listopia and email list growth compound over 60 to 90 days.

Can you guarantee that my book will become a bestseller? +

No honest book marketer can guarantee specific sales numbers or bestseller status, as results depend on book quality, cover appeal, genre competition, and reader timing. I guarantee proven, data driven execution, continuous optimization, and radical transparency.
