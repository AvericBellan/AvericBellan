Book Launch Strategy Published: January 15, 2026 Reading Time: 12 min

The Ultimate 90 Day Book Launch Checklist for 2026

Your book launch is the single most important event in your book's entire life cycle. The decisions you make and the actions you take in the 90 days surrounding your publication date will determine whether your book becomes a visible, profitable, review rich bestseller or whether it disappears into the Amazon void within the first week.

90 to 60 Days Before Launch

Finalize your manuscript, cover design, and interior formatting. Do not leave any of these critical elements to the last minute. Set up your Amazon Author Central profile, create your Goodreads Author Profile, and identify your top 10 comp titles on Amazon for keyword targeting. Build your reader magnet, commission professional 3D book mockups, begin scripting your book trailer, and compile a list of the top 20 Goodreads Listopia lists in your genre.

59 to 30 Days Before Launch

Begin your ARC recruitment campaign targeting 100 to 200 readers using BookSirens, BookSprout, and NetGalley. Write your 7 to 12 email launch sequence, begin your social media teasers, finalize book trailer production, nominate your book to Goodreads Listopia lists, and start personalized Book Club Listopia outreach. Build your Amazon Ads campaign structure and keep it paused until launch day.

29 Days to Launch Day

Send reminder emails to your ARC readers, finalize promotional stacking across deal sites, test Amazon Ads at low budgets for 48 hours, schedule launch social media posts, and confirm book club reading selections.

Launch Day and Beyond

Activate full Amazon Ad budgets, send launch announcement emails, monitor ranking velocity, optimize ads daily, maintain Listopia voting momentum, and transition into sustained long term scaling 30 days post launch.

Amazon Advertising Published: February 3, 2026 Reading Time: 15 min

Amazon Ads for Authors: A Complete Beginner's Guide to Profitable Campaigns

Amazon Ads put your book directly in front of readers who are actively searching for their next read at the exact moment they are ready to buy. However, many authors burn through hundreds of dollars due to unoptimized bids, bloated keyword lists, and a lack of negative targeting.

Mastering Sponsored Products

Sponsored Products are the foundation of any author ad strategy. Focus on targeted comp titles, specific author names, and niche genre keywords. Separate your campaigns by match type (broad, phrase, and exact) to retain total control over your cost per click.

The Power of Negative Keywords

Add negative keywords immediately to eliminate wasted spend on irrelevant searches (for example, adding dark romance or paranormal if you write clean contemporary romance). Review your search term reports weekly to add new negative keywords.

Conservative Bidding Strategies

Start with bids between $0.25 and $0.50 for fiction and $0.35 and $0.75 for non fiction. Allow at least two weeks of data to accumulate before making bid adjustments. Optimize continuously to keep your ACOS below your target threshold.

Reviews and Social Proof Published: March 10, 2026 Reading Time: 10 min

How to Get 100 Reviews on Amazon in Your First 30 Days Without Breaking the Rules

Books with 50 or more verified reviews convert at dramatically higher rates than books with fewer than 10. Getting to 100 reviews in your first 30 days is entirely achievable with a systematic, compliant process.

Recruit 150 to 200 ARC readers across BookSirens, BookSprout, and NetGalley 60 to 90 days prior to launch. Follow up with polite, non coercive review request emails upon launch, leverage your email list subscribers, and use Amazon's built in Request a Review button for every eligible order.
