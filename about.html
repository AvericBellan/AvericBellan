Get to Know the Strategist Behind Your Next Bestseller Campaign

I Am Averic Bellan, a Full Service Book Marketing Specialist and Strategist Who Has Helped Over 200 Authors Across 15 Genres Turn Their Books Into Bestsellers, Build Thriving Reader Communities, and Create Sustainable Marketing Systems That Generate Sales Long After Launch Day.

I did not come from the traditional publishing world. I came from digital marketing, data analysis, and performance advertising. And that outside perspective is exactly what gives me the edge that most book marketers simply do not have. I understand algorithms, consumer psychology, conversion optimization, and ROI driven strategy at a level that goes far beyond simply knowing how to boost a Facebook post or run a basic Amazon ad. I combine that deep technical expertise with a genuine, lifelong passion for books and reading, and the result is a marketing approach that is both analytically rigorous and creatively inspired.

How I Went From Digital Marketer to the Book Marketing Strategist Authors Trust With Their Careers

I did not start my career in publishing, and I think that is actually my greatest advantage. I started in digital marketing, running paid advertising campaigns across Google, Facebook, Instagram, and Amazon for e commerce brands, SaaS companies, and online course creators. I spent years mastering the art and science of customer acquisition, conversion rate optimization, funnel architecture, email automation, and data driven decision making. I understood how algorithms worked, how consumer psychology influenced purchasing decisions, and how to turn a modest advertising budget into a significant return on investment long before I ever touched a book launch.

But everything changed when a close friend came to me with a problem. She had just finished writing her debut novel, a contemporary romance she had spent three years pouring her heart into. The manuscript was polished, the cover was gorgeous, and she was incredibly proud of what she had created. But she had absolutely no idea how to market it. She was about to publish it on Amazon KDP and simply hope that readers would find it organically. I told her that was a terrible plan, and I offered to help.

I dove headfirst into the world of book marketing with the same intensity and analytical rigor I brought to every other marketing challenge in my career. I studied Amazon's A9 search algorithm until I understood exactly how it ranked books and what factors influenced visibility. I learned everything there was to know about KDP Select, Kindle Unlimited page reads, and category optimization. I researched BookBub ad mechanics, BookTok content trends, ARC team management best practices, email list building strategies for authors, Goodreads Listopia dynamics, book club outreach protocols, book mockup design principles, and book trailer production workflows. I consumed every resource, case study, and data set I could find.

Then I built her a complete launch campaign from scratch. I recruited an ARC team of 100 readers. I set up a reader magnet funnel that captured 800 email subscribers in three weeks. I launched a targeted Amazon Ads campaign focused on high converting comp titles in her sub genre. I designed professional 3D book mockups for her landing page and social media posts. I nominated her book to 10 Goodreads Listopia lists and pitched it to 5 romance book clubs. I coordinated a BookTok influencer campaign with 8 content creators in her niche.

The result? Her book hit the Top 100 in its Amazon category within the first week. She received over 80 verified reviews in the first 30 days. She sold more copies in her launch month than most debut authors sell in their entire first year. And she did it all without a single video call or meeting. Everything was coordinated through detailed, organized email exchanges that kept both of us aligned and accountable throughout the entire process.

After that launch, other authors started reaching out to me. Then more authors. Then small indie publishers. Then literary agencies. And before I knew it, book marketing was not just a favor for a friend. It was my full time mission, my primary business, and my deepest professional passion.

Today, I have helped over 200 authors across fiction and non fiction genres launch, scale, and sustain their book sales. I have managed campaigns for debut novelists, established thriller writers, self help gurus, business thought leaders, memoirists, fantasy world builders, and everything in between. I have seen every possible scenario, navigated every conceivable challenge, and built strategies that work for every single stage of the author journey. And I have done it all through clear, documented, organized email communication that keeps my clients informed, empowered, and confident at every step.

What I Believe About Book Marketing, Author Success, and the Future of Publishing

Every book deserves an audience. Not just any audience, but the right audience. The readers who will devour your words at 2 AM, leave glowing five star reviews that convince strangers to take a chance on your work, and immediately text their best friend saying they absolutely have to read your book. Finding those readers and connecting them with your book is not a matter of luck or wishful thinking. It is a matter of strategy, execution, and persistence. And that is exactly what I provide.

I do not believe in one size fits all marketing. A steamy romance novel launch looks nothing like a business leadership book launch, and a debut author with zero platform needs a completely different approach than a 10 book veteran with a 50,000 person email list and an established backlist. That is why every single campaign I build is custom tailored to your unique goals, genre, target audience, competitive landscape, and budget. I take the time to understand your specific situation before I recommend a single tactic, and I adjust my strategies continuously based on real time data and performance metrics.

I also believe in radical transparency and complete documentation. You will never wonder where your money is going, what campaigns are running, what optimizations have been made, or what results we are achieving. I provide clear weekly email updates, detailed monthly performance reports, and honest, straightforward communication at every step of the process. Because I conduct all of my business through Gmail, you will always have a complete, searchable, time stamped record of every conversation, every strategy decision, every campaign adjustment, and every performance report. Nothing gets lost. Nothing gets forgotten. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Finally, I believe that marketing should amplify your book, not overshadow it. The best marketing campaign in the world cannot save a poorly written book with a terrible cover and a confusing blurb. But the right marketing strategy can turn a genuinely great book into a cultural phenomenon, a bestseller, and a career defining achievement. My job is not to hype up mediocre work. My job is to make sure that excellent work gets the spotlight, the visibility, and the readership it truly deserves.

The Types of Authors and Publishing Professionals I Work With Most Frequently

  • Debut Authors Preparing for Their First Launch: If you are about to publish your first book and you feel completely overwhelmed by the marketing process, you are not alone. I help debut authors build complete launch plans from scratch, including ARC campaigns, Amazon Ads setup, email funnel creation, book mockup design, book trailer production, Goodreads Listopia nominations, and Book Club Listopia outreach.
  • Indie and Self Published Authors Looking to Scale: If you have one or more books already published but your sales have plateaued and you cannot seem to break through to the next level, I can help you identify exactly what is holding you back and implement the specific strategies needed to reignite your growth.
  • Traditionally Published Authors Seeking More Control: Many traditionally published authors come to me because their publisher's in house marketing team did the bare minimum. I help these authors take control of their own marketing destiny by building independent campaigns that complement or surpass what their publisher provided.
  • Non Fiction Authors, Coaches, and Thought Leaders: If you wrote a non fiction book to establish authority, attract clients, grow your brand, or open doors to speaking opportunities, I understand that your marketing goals are fundamentally different from a fiction author's goals.
  • Series Authors Maximizing Backlist Revenue: If you have a series of three or more books, you are sitting on a goldmine of untapped revenue potential. I help series authors optimize their read through rates through strategic bundling, cross promotional advertising, and sustained Amazon Ads campaigns.
  • Small Publishers and Literary Agencies: If you are a publisher or agency looking for a reliable, results driven marketing partner you can trust with your authors' careers, I offer white label and collaborative marketing services that integrate seamlessly with your existing operations.
