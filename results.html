From Zero Platform and Zero Marketing Experience to Number One New Release on Amazon in Just 14 Days

Client: Author S. M. | Romance Author | Debut Author | First Book

The Challenge

Author S. M. came to me exactly eight weeks before her planned publication date with a completed and professionally edited manuscript, a beautiful cover designed by a freelance artist, and absolutely zero marketing infrastructure in place. She had no email list, no social media following beyond a personal account with a few hundred friends, no ARC team, no advertising experience, and no Goodreads author profile. She was terrified that her book was going to launch into complete silence and disappear forever.

The Strategy

We built a rapid but comprehensive 60 day launch plan from scratch, coordinated entirely through detailed email exchanges. I recruited and managed an ARC team of 150 readers through BookSirens and direct outreach. I set up a reader magnet funnel featuring a bonus epilogue that captured 1,400 email subscribers during pre launch. I coordinated a BookTok campaign with 12 romance creators, launched a targeted Amazon Ads campaign on 50 comp titles, designed professional 3D mockups, produced a 45 second book trailer, nominated her book to 18 Goodreads Listopia lists, and secured 3 confirmed romance book club selections.

The Quantifiable Results

  • Number one New Release in Contemporary Romance on Amazon within 14 days of publication.
  • 127 verified Amazon reviews within the first 30 days, with an average rating of 4.6 stars.
  • 3,200+ copies sold in the launch month across Kindle, paperback, and Kindle Unlimited page reads.
  • 1,400 email subscribers acquired during the pre launch phase for future releases.
  • Selected for a BookBub Featured Deal within 60 days, generating an additional 8,000 downloads in 24 hours.
  • Book trailer generated over 15,000 views on YouTube in the first two weeks.
  • Goodreads Listopia placements drove an estimated 2,000 additional profile visits and 450 shelf additions.
  • Book club selections generated 180 bulk copy sales and sustained organic word of mouth.

Cutting ACOS by 58 Percent While Simultaneously Doubling Monthly Sales and Reviving a Stagnant Backlist

Client: Author J. T. | Thriller Author | 5 Book Backlist | 2 Years Self Managed Ads

The Challenge

Author J. T. had been running Amazon Ads on his own for over a year, spending roughly $2,000 per month across his five book backlist. His ACOS was hovering around 85 percent, meaning he was losing money on nearly every sale. His campaigns were bloated with over 4,000 unoptimized keywords, some bidding $3.50 per click without a single sale. He had zero negative keyword strategy in place, his ad creatives were flat and unappealing, and his ad spend was bleeding heavily every month.

The Strategy

I performed a comprehensive audit of his entire advertising account and restructured everything from the ground up. I eliminated over 2,000 wasted keywords, implemented aggressive negative keyword lists totaling over 500 terms, reorganized campaign architecture by match type, tested new Sponsored Brand and Lockscreen formats, redesigned his book mockups for use in ad creatives, and optimized his Amazon book descriptions and A+ Content.

The Quantifiable Results

  • ACOS dropped from 85 percent to 36 percent within 45 days, turning ads profitable.
  • Monthly ad driven sales increased by 112 percent despite spending $600 less per month.
  • Backlist sales grew by 67 percent in 90 days due to optimized series read through.
  • Monthly ad spend reduced from $2,000 to $1,400 while generating significantly higher revenue.
  • Overall ROI on ad spend improved by 340 percent across all active campaigns.
  • New 3D book mockups increased Sponsored Brand ad click through rates by 38 percent.

50,000 Downloads in One Week, a USA Today Bestseller, and a Complete Career Transformation

Client: Author P. K. | Self Help Author | Traditionally Published | 6 Months Post Publication

The Challenge

Author P. K.'s book had been published by a mid size traditional publisher with minimal marketing support. Sales had completely flatlined after launch week, her Amazon ranking had dropped to number 45,000, and she was receiving fewer than 10 sales per week. She was deeply frustrated that her book was not reaching the readers who needed it most.

The Strategy

I executed a comprehensive revival campaign. I secured a BookBub Featured Deal at $0.99, coordinated multi channel promotions across Freebooksy and Bargain Booksy, ran targeted lookalike Facebook Ads, pitched Author P. K. to 15 top personal development podcasts (landing 8 interviews in 30 days), executed a Goodreads Listopia campaign across 22 relevant lists, and conducted Book Club Listopia outreach to professional reading circles.

The Quantifiable Results

  • 50,000+ downloads in a single 7 day promotional window.
  • Earned a place on the prestigious USA Today Bestseller list.
  • 8 podcast appearances completed with combined audience reach exceeding 500,000 downloads.
  • Speaking and consulting inquiries tripled within 60 days.
  • Amazon ranking improved from #45,000 to #120 in primary category, staying in top 500 for six months.
  • Goodreads Listopia generated sustained organic sales of 200 to 400 copies monthly with no ongoing ad spend.
  • Book club selections generated over 800 bulk copy sales and three corporate speaking gigs worth $15,000.
