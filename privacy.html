Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Averic Bellan Book Marketing, your privacy and the security of your personal information are extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, interact with our services, download free resources, or communicate with us through Gmail.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your full name, email address, book title, genre, and details about your marketing needs when you fill out our contact form or send us an email via Gmail. We do not collect credit card numbers or sensitive personal financial data through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for the following purposes: to communicate with you about our book marketing services, to deliver custom proposals and marketing strategies, to send you relevant educational resources, and to improve our website offerings.

Data Sharing and Privacy

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes under any circumstances. Limited technical data may be processed through trusted form submission platforms (such as Formspree) solely to deliver your message to our inbox.

Data Storage and Security

All client communications and campaign records are stored securely within Gmail with two factor authentication and industry standard encryption. Access is restricted exclusively to Averic Bellan.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, correct, or request the deletion of your personal information at any time. Simply send an email request to avericbellanbookmarketing@gmail.com and your request will be processed promptly.

Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email avericbellanbookmarketing@gmail.com.
